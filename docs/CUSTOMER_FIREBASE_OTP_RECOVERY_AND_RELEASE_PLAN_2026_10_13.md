## Outcome and release boundaries
- Complete Firebase **Customer Android** phone login/signup, preserve original Supabase profile/customer/membership UUIDs, documents and business records.
- Each customer may also be an employee under the *same* verified phone, but never merge roles, map Firebase identity to a staff profile, transfer customer data, or grant staff permissions to a customer token. Staff/partner/web Supabase Auth remains unchanged.
- Do **not** globally disable Supabase Auth or delete legacy customers, identities, passwords/sessions, 2Factor functions or SMS templates. Retire **Customer App's legacy SMS OTP** only after safe Firebase cutover with a tested rollback. Audit other consumers of 2Factor separately before later decommissioning.
- Production-only Supabase is the sole DB; no fake preview DB assumptions. Prefer read-only inspection and reversible additive migrations guarded by explicit approval, with row-count / ownership preflight and rollback. Do not run exploratory writes against the two real user accounts.
- **No APK**. Native Firebase is already in internal-test AAB `0.3.1 (10)`, so use runtime-compatible Android 0.3.1 OTA for JS-only candidate corrections when feasible. Any new native binary needs a new Play `versionCode` because 10 has already been uploaded to Internal Testing; propose `0.3.2 (11)`, subject to release approval. No Google Play uploads without explicit approval.
